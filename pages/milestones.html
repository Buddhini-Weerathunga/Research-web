Project Milestones

View the key assessments, submission dates, and allocated marks for each project milestone.

Project Proposal

The project proposal assessment evaluates the initial research idea, problem background, objectives, scope, literature review, methodology, and feasibility of the proposed solution.

Date: 2025-08-15 Marks: 12%

Progress Presentation 1

This assessment reviews the first stage of implementation, including system design, research progress, completed modules, technical approach, and planned improvements.

Date: 2025-10-10 Marks: 15%

Progress Presentation 2

This assessment focuses on advanced implementation progress, model development, testing results, system integration, and individual contribution of each team member.

Date: 2026-01-20 Marks: 18%

Research Paper

Includes the final research manuscript with literature, methodology, implementation, evaluation results, and conclusions prepared for formal submission.

Date: 2026-04-05 Marks: Included in Final Assessment

Final Report & Presentation

Contains the complete project report and final presentation materials summarizing research outcomes, implementation details, and overall contribution.

Date: 2026-04-05 Marks: Included in Final Assessment

CheckList Documents

Submission checklists and supporting verification documents required to confirm completeness and compliance with assessment requirements.

Date: 2026-04-05 Marks: Included in Final Assessment
