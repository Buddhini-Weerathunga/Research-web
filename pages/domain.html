Research Domain

AI-Powered Personalized Traditional Medicine Assistant

❧ ✦ ❧

Domain details gathered through literature survey, research analysis, methodology, and technologies.

❧✦

Domain Details

Research Group: 25-26J-232

01

Literature Survey

Existing research shows that artificial intelligence, machine learning, image processing, predictive analytics, and augmented reality are increasingly used in healthcare systems. However, most existing systems mainly focus on modern medical diagnosis and do not provide strong support for Ayurveda-based personalized healthcare.

Literature also highlights the importance of personalized diagnosis, medicinal plant safety, health forecasting, and guided therapy support. Therefore, this project focuses on combining traditional medicine knowledge with modern AI technologies to improve accuracy, safety, and accessibility.

02

Research Gap

Current digital healthcare platforms do not provide a complete integrated solution for traditional medicine. Most available systems handle only one function, such as symptom checking, plant identification, or health tracking.

There is a clear gap in combining dosha diagnosis, medicinal plant identification, safety alerts, health profile forecasting, diet recommendations, and AR-based therapy guidance into one intelligent Ayurveda-based assistant.

03

Research Problem

Many users face difficulties in receiving accurate personalized traditional medicine guidance. They may not know their dosha imbalance, the safe use of medicinal plants, future health risks, or the correct way to perform traditional therapy procedures.

The research problem focuses on how artificial intelligence can be used to improve personalization, safety, accessibility, and preventive healthcare in Ayurveda-based traditional medicine.

04

Research Objectives

The main objective is to develop an AI-powered personalized traditional medicine assistant that supports Ayurveda-based health analysis and guidance.

  • Identify Vata, Pitta, and Kapha imbalances using AI.
  • Recommend personalized traditional medicine prescriptions.
  • Identify medicinal plants and provide safety alerts.
  • Analyze health profiles and forecast future health risks.
  • Guide users through therapy procedures using AR technology.
05

Methodology

The system collects user health data, lifestyle details, symptoms, environmental information, and plant images. These inputs are processed through AI and machine learning models to generate personalized health insights.

The system uses separate modules for dosha diagnosis, medicinal plant identification, health profile forecasting, diet recommendation, and AR-based therapy guidance. Results are displayed through a user-friendly web interface.

06

Technologies Used

  • MERN Stack: Web application development
  • Python: AI and machine learning model development
  • XGBoost: Health forecasting and prediction
  • Image Processing: Medicinal plant identification
  • AR Technology: Therapy guidance system
  • Database: User profiles, health data, and prediction results
