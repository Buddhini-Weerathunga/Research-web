Research Domain

AI-Powered Personalized Traditional Medicine Assistant

❧ ✦ ❧

Domain details gathered through literature survey, research analysis, methodology, and technologies.

01

Literature Survey

Recent advancements in Artificial Intelligence (AI), Machine Learning (ML), Computer Vision, and Augmented Reality (AR) have significantly transformed modern healthcare systems. Numerous studies have demonstrated the effectiveness of AI models such as Random Forest, XGBoost, and deep learning techniques in disease prediction, health monitoring, and decision support systems.

In addition, image processing and deep learning models have been successfully applied in medicinal plant identification, while Natural Language Processing (NLP) and chatbot systems have improved automated health data collection and user interaction. AR technologies are also being used to guide users in medical training and therapy procedures.

However, most existing systems primarily focus on modern medical practices and lack integration with traditional medicine systems such as Ayurveda. There is limited research on combining personalized diagnosis, medicinal plant safety, predictive health forecasting, and guided therapy support within a single intelligent platform. Therefore, this project aims to bridge this gap by integrating traditional Ayurvedic knowledge with modern AI technologies to enhance accuracy, safety, and accessibility in healthcare.

02

Research Gap

Although various AI-based healthcare applications have been developed, most existing systems focus on isolated functionalities such as symptom checking, disease prediction, medicinal plant identification, or health monitoring. These solutions often lack a holistic approach and do not effectively integrate multiple aspects of personalized healthcare.

Furthermore, there is limited integration of traditional medicine systems, particularly Ayurveda, with modern Artificial Intelligence technologies. Existing platforms rarely consider key Ayurvedic concepts such as dosha analysis (Vata, Pitta, Kapha), lifestyle patterns, and environmental factors when providing recommendations.

In addition, there is a noticeable absence of systems that combine multimodal data sources—including user inputs, image data, and environmental data—to deliver comprehensive health insights. Features such as real-time safety alerts for medicinal plant usage, predictive health forecasting, personalized diet recommendations, and AR-based therapy guidance are rarely unified within a single platform.

Therefore, a significant research gap exists in developing an integrated, intelligent, and user-friendly system that combines AI, machine learning, computer vision, and augmented reality with traditional Ayurvedic knowledge to provide personalized, predictive, and preventive healthcare solutions.

03

Research Problem

Traditional medicine systems such as Ayurveda rely heavily on expert knowledge and manual assessment, making them less accessible, time-consuming, and difficult to scale for a wider population. At the same time, modern digital healthcare solutions often lack personalization, preventive focus, and integration with traditional medical practices.

The core research problem addressed in this project is how to design and develop an intelligent system that integrates Artificial Intelligence with Ayurvedic knowledge to provide accurate, personalized, and predictive healthcare support. This includes enabling automated dosha diagnosis, safe medicinal plant usage, health profile analysis, and guided therapy in a single unified platform.

04

Research Objectives

The primary objective of this research is to develop an AI-powered personalized traditional medicine assistant that enhances Ayurveda-based healthcare by providing accurate, predictive, and user-friendly health analysis and guidance.

  • To identify Vata, Pitta, and Kapha imbalances using machine learning techniques.
  • To provide personalized traditional medicine recommendations based on individual health profiles.
  • To develop a medicinal plant identification system with real-time safety alerts and usage guidance.
  • To analyze user health data and forecast potential health risks using predictive models.
  • To implement an AR-based therapy guidance system to assist users in performing treatments correctly.
  • To integrate multiple AI technologies into a single intelligent and user-friendly healthcare platform.
05

Methodology

The proposed system follows a modular and integrated methodology that combines data collection, machine learning, computer vision, and augmented reality technologies to deliver personalized healthcare solutions based on Ayurvedic principles. The development process begins with collecting user health data, including lifestyle habits, dietary patterns, and environmental factors, along with image data for medicinal plant identification.

The collected data is preprocessed and analyzed using machine learning models such as XGBoost and Random Forest to identify dosha imbalances, generate health profiles, and forecast potential health risks. Deep learning and computer vision techniques are applied to accurately identify medicinal plants and provide safety alerts. The system is integrated using a React-based frontend, a Node.js backend for API management, and Python-based AI services developed with FastAPI. Additionally, an AR-based module is implemented to guide users through therapy procedures, ensuring accurate and safe application of treatments.

06

Technologies Used

  • Frontend (React + Vite): Developed a fast and responsive user interface using React.js, Vite, Tailwind CSS, and React Router DOM for navigation.
  • Backend (Node.js & Express): Implemented RESTful APIs, request handling, and system integration using Node.js and Express.js.
  • Database (MongoDB & Mongoose): Used MongoDB for storing user profiles, health records, and prediction results, with Mongoose for schema management.
  • AI & Machine Learning (Python - FastAPI): Built AI services using FastAPI with libraries such as Scikit-learn, XGBoost, NumPy, and Pandas for health analysis and forecasting.
  • Deep Learning & Computer Vision: Applied OpenCV, MediaPipe, PyTorch, and TensorFlow for medicinal plant identification, pose detection, and image analysis.
  • AR & Pose Detection: Integrated AR-based therapy guidance using MediaPipe Pose, TensorFlow.js, and React Webcam for real-time user interaction.
  • Security & Authentication: Implemented secure authentication using JSON Web Tokens (JWT), Bcrypt for password hashing, and middleware such as Helmet and Express Rate Limit.
  • External APIs: Integrated OpenWeatherMap API for environmental data such as weather and location-based insights.
  • Development & Tools: Used tools such as GitHub, Google Colab (for model training), ESLint, PostCSS, Autoprefixer, and Nodemon for efficient development and deployment.
